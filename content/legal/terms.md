## 8. What we can and cannot promise about the result

The models that draw your picture are run by third parties and are not
deterministic. The same photograph and the same style can produce different
results on different days, and a result may be unflattering, wrong, or simply not
what you pictured.

The examples shown in the catalogue illustrate a style. They are not a promise
that your own photograph will produce a similar picture: that depends on your
photograph — its light, its framing, how much of the face is visible.

Because of this, a result you dislike is not by itself a fault. What we do
undertake is narrower and firmer: **if a generation fails, you get your TOONTOON
back** (Section 4).
